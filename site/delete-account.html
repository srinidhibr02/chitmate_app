Delete your ChitMate account

App: ChitMate (app.zerolpa.chit_mate)

In the app

Open Settings → Delete account and confirm.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email srinidhibr02@gmail.com from any address with the subject “Delete my ChitMate account” and the mobile number you signed in with. We will confirm the number and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in record (your mobile number).
  • Every group you organise, with its members, chits, votes, results and activity log.
  • Groups you only belong to are run by someone else and are not deleted. Ask that organiser to remove you, or email us and we will remove your name and number from them.
  • Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

Deletion cannot be undone. See the privacy policy for details.
