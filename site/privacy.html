ChitMate Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · App: ChitMate (app.zerolpa.chit_mate)

ChitMate helps friends, family and colleagues keep track of a chit fund / savings circle: members, chits, monthly voting, results and who has paid. ChitMate does not hold, collect, send or lend money. It is a record-keeping and organising tool only. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Who is responsible

ChitMate is published by ZeroLPA (“we”, “us”). For anything in this policy, write to srinidhibr02@gmail.com.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Your mobile number, when you sign in with a one-time codeTo sign you in, keep you signed in, and match you to the groups that list your number.
Names and mobile numbers of members, typed in by a group organiserTo show who is in the group and to let that person see their group after they sign in with the same number. If you are added by someone else, they entered your details, not you.
Group records: chit value, chits and shares, votes, monthly results, payment status marks, and an activity logTo run the group: work out payouts, show results and keep a history.
Settings stored on your device (for example reminder preferences)To schedule reminders and remember your choices.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, bank or card details, or any government ID. The app shows no advertising and we do not sell your information.

How it is stored and who handles it

  • Sign-in and storage use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Google acts on our behalf as a service provider. Group data is stored in Google’s India (Mumbai) region.
  • One-time codes are sent by SMS through Google’s phone-verification service. Your mobile carrier may charge normal SMS rates.
  • Reminders are created on your own device and are not sent through our servers.
  • Data is encrypted in transit. Access rules allow an organiser to change their group and allow listed members to view it.

Who can see your information

Other people in the same group can see the group’s records, including member names, mobile numbers as entered, chits, votes and results. Only the group organiser can change the group. We do not share your information with anyone else except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and group records while the account is active. When you delete your account (see below), we delete your sign-in record and every group you organise, with its members, chits, votes, results and activity log. In a group that someone else organises, the organiser entered your name and number, so that group’s record keeps them until the organiser removes you (organisers can do this in the app) or you email us and we remove them. Backups are overwritten within a short period.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data: use Delete account in the app’s settings, or see how to request deletion.
  • Access or correct your information: an organiser can correct member details in the app; or email us.
  • Withdraw consent to us processing your number by deleting your account.
  • Complaints: write to the email above and we will reply within 30 days. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

ChitMate is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Not financial advice or a financial service

ChitMate does not run chit funds, accept deposits, give loans or guarantee payments. Any money between group members is arranged and paid outside the app. Group arrangements may be subject to the Chit Funds Act, 1982 and state rules, and are the responsibility of the people who run them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
